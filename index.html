Let PogoBot Sleep

Bugs are crawling towards PogoBot. Type their words to squash them before they wake him up.

  • Start typing a bug's word and you lock onto it. Finish the word to squash it.
  • Locked onto the wrong one? Press Esc to let go.
  • When the spray can floats past, type pogo to grab it.
  • PogoBot has 3 snoozes. Each bug that reaches him uses one, and the third one wakes him up.
